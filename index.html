    Controls

    Desktop: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Click an atom to inspect it, shift-click more atoms to measure distance (2), angle (3) or dihedral (4). Double-click an atom to orbit around it. Space play/pause, ←/→ step, Esc clear, R reset view, F focus selection, P screenshot.
    N/B next/previous lesson stop.
    VR: look at the menu on your left controller and point at it with the right one to play, scrub the timeline, step through lesson stops, or change style, color, water, H-bonds, arrows and paths. Grip to grab and move; grip with both hands to scale and rotate. Trigger picks atoms (up to 4 for measuring; trigger on empty space clears). Shortcuts: A/X play/pause, B/Y style, stick click resets the view, right stick ←→ scrub / ↑↓ speed, left stick ←→ coloring / ↑↓ next/previous stop (water, if no stops).
